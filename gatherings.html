Occasions

What people celebrate here

Riva is small, so your group isn't one of many. Here's what we host most often.

Birthdays

Cake under the lights, a barbecue dinner and the kids running round the garden.

Family reunions

Spread the family across the cottages and come together for meals.

Anniversaries

A quiet dinner for two or a small evening with close friends.

Group getaways

Friends, colleagues or a club, with space to eat, play and talk late.

How it works

Three simple steps

No event planners or packages to decode. Just a conversation with us.

  1. Tell us the occasionSend the date, your group size and what you're celebrating, using the form below or WhatsApp.
  2. We plan it togetherWe'll suggest rooms, meals and extras, and send you a clear quote.
  3. Arrive and celebrateEverything's ready when you get here. You just enjoy the day.
