Swimming pool

A pool in the trees

The pool sits on the hillside, framed by forest, with a view out over the green slopes. There's a slide for the kids and log-style seats around the edge for the grown-ups. After dark, the lights come on and it glows blue against the trees.

Play and games

Room for the kids to run

The play area is tucked into the gardens beside the pool: covered swings, a seesaw and the slide, all on soft ground and inside the fence. When it rains, the indoor games room keeps everyone busy.
