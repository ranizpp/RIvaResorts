Finding us

The last stretch

Riva is just off the main road through Vythiri. Here's what to look out for as you arrive.

  1. Head for VythiriFollow NH 766 into Wayanad toward Vythiri, whether you're coming up the ghat from Kozhikode or down from Mysuru.
  2. Turn onto Pookode Lake RoadLook for the green gate posts and the Riva sign under the bamboo. Nearby landmark
  3. Park insideDrive in through the gate. There's free, secure parking right in front of the main building.
